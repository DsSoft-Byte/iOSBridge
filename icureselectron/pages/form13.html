DFU Mode — Step by Step

Device Firmware Update
DFU bypasses iBoot stage one entirely — the lowest-level restore state. Required for tether-downgrades, jailbreaks, and devices that won't enter recovery.
iPhone X / 11 / 12 / 13 / 14 / 15 (Face ID)
1

2
Hold Side button until screen goes black (~3 s)
3
While holding Side, also press Volume Down for 5 s
4
Release Side only — keep holding Volume Down 5 more seconds
iPhone 7 / 8 / SE 2nd Gen (Side + Volume Down)
1
Hold Side + Volume Down simultaneously for 8 seconds
2

3

Pwned DFU

checkm8 / limera1n exploit
Sends the exploit payload while in DFU mode, disabling signature verification. Required for checkra1n / palera1n jailbreaks and unsigned IPSW restores on A5–A11 devices.
